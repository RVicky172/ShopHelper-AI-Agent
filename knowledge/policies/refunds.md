# Refund Policy

- Refunds are only issued for orders that have been **delivered** and are within the **30-day refund window** (counted from the delivery date).
- Orders that have not shipped yet should be **cancelled** instead of refunded (see the Cancellation Policy).
- Approved refunds go back to the **original payment method** within **5-7 business days**. We do not issue cash refunds.
- **Every refund requires approval by a human support agent** before it is processed. ShopHelper's assistant can submit a refund request but cannot approve it.
- Refunds above **$200** are additionally reviewed by a support supervisor.
- Partial refunds are possible, for example when an item is returned without its accessories.
- An order can only be refunded once.
