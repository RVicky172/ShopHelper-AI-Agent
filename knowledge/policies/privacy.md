# Privacy and Data Policy

- Chat transcripts with the ShopHelper assistant are kept for **30 days** and then deleted automatically.
- We only use personal data to fulfil orders and provide support. We do not sell personal data.
- To request a copy or deletion of your data, email **privacy@shophelper.example**; requests are completed within 30 days.
- The assistant automatically masks or blocks sensitive data such as card numbers and government IDs.
