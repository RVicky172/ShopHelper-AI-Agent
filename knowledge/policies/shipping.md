# Shipping Policy (Domestic)

- Orders are processed within **1 business day**.
- **Standard shipping** takes 3-5 business days and is **free on orders over $50**; otherwise it costs **$4.99**.
- **Express shipping** takes 1-2 business days and costs **$12.99**.
- A tracking link is emailed as soon as the order ships. Order status values are: processing, shipped, delivered, cancelled, refunded.
- If a package shows as delivered but has not arrived, wait 48 hours and then contact support; we will open a carrier investigation.
- We cannot change the shipping address after an order has shipped.
