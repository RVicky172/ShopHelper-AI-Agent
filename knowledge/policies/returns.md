# Returns Policy

- Most items can be returned within **30 days of delivery**.
- Items must be undamaged and include all accessories and the original packaging.
- Opened electronics that are not defective can be returned but carry a **10% restocking fee**.
- Defective items have **no restocking fee** and we provide a free prepaid return label.
- For non-defective returns, a prepaid return label costs **$5.99** and is deducted from the refund.
- **Final-sale items cannot be returned**: gift cards, clearance items marked "Final Sale", and downloadable software.
- To start a return, contact support with your order ID. Returns are inspected within 3 business days of arrival at our warehouse.
