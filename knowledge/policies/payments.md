# Payment Methods

- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI and PayPal.
- Payment is charged when the order is placed.
- **ShopHelper support will never ask for your full card number, CVV, OTP or password** - in chat, email or by phone. Never share them.
- If a payment fails, the order is not created; please retry or use another method.
- Prices are shown in USD and include applicable sales tax at checkout.
