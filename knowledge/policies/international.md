# International Shipping

- We ship to **25 countries**, including India, the UK, Canada, Australia, Germany and Singapore.
- International delivery takes **7-14 business days**.
- **Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer** and are not refundable.
- Items containing lithium batteries above 100Wh cannot be shipped internationally.
- International returns follow the same 30-day window, but the customer pays return shipping unless the item is defective.
