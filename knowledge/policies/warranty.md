# Warranty Policy

- All electronics include a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date covering manufacturing defects.
- **ShopHelper Care** extends coverage to **2 years** for **$19.99** and must be purchased within 30 days of delivery.
- Warranties do **not** cover accidental damage, water damage, unauthorised repairs or normal wear.
- For a warranty claim, contact support with the order ID and a short description or photo of the defect. We repair or replace the item; if neither is possible we issue store credit.
