# Warranty Policy

- **Electronics** means everything in audio, keyboards and mice, laptop accessories, phone accessories, and power and charging, including items such as phone cases, card wallets and desk mats.
- All electronics include a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date covering manufacturing defects.
- **Lifestyle products** (home and kitchen, bags and travel, fitness and outdoors: for example kettles, mugs, backpacks, suitcases, yoga mats and kettlebells) include a **90-day warranty** from the delivery date against defects.
- **ShopHelper Care** is for **electronics only**: audio, keyboards and mice, laptop accessories (such as laptop stands), phone accessories, and power and charging, including phone cases, card wallets and desk mats. It extends coverage to **2 years** for **$19.99** and must be purchased within 30 days of delivery. It is not available for lifestyle products.
- Warranties do **not** cover accidental damage, water damage, unauthorised repairs or normal wear.
- For a warranty claim, contact support with the order ID and a short description or photo of the defect. We repair or replace the item; if neither is possible we issue store credit.
